04Process

From first line to final load.

A good structure is a sequence of good decisions. I stay close to every one of them — from the initial constraint map to the final site inspection.

MV

Every detail
has a reason.

  1. 01

    Interrogate

    Weeks 01—04

    Site, climate, constraints, cost, ambition. Before drawing a line, we map the forces already at play.

  2. 02

    Resolve

    Weeks 05—14

    Physical models, parametric studies, load paths. We test many structures to find the one that feels inevitable.

  3. 03

    Document

    Weeks 15—36

    Coordinated geometry, honest details and clear drawings. The poetry has to survive the tender.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Construction

    Site reviews, prototyping, testing, handover. The drawing becomes a place; I make sure it still adds up.
